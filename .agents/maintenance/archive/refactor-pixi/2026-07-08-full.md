# refactor-pixi — 2026-07-08 — WS population invalidation

## Theme

Fix missing `population` and `activeExpeditions` query invalidations across 9 WS event handlers in `ws-bindings.ts`. Troops returning/moving change village population counts, but the TanStack Query cache was not invalidated, leaving the HUD population bar stale until the next poll cycle.

## Root causes

1. **`invalidateReinforcementQueries` helper** covered `activeExpeditions`, `garrison`, `armyInventory` per village but omitted `population`. All 4 callers (reinforcement.sent/recalled/returned, garrison.added) inherited the gap.

2. **Three "returned" handlers** (`battle.returned`, `scout.returned`, `expedition.returned`) each manually invalidated `armyInventory` and sometimes `resources`, but skipped `population`. Fix: use `invalidateVillageEconomy` (resources+population) where resources are also needed, add bare `population` for scout.returned (no resource change).

3. **All "returned" handlers** (battle/scout/expedition/caravan) skipped `activeExpeditions(villageId)` while their "sent" counterparts included it. The village-level expedition panel stayed stale.
